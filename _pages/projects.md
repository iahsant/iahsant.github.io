<div class="card research-card mb-4"><div class="row no-gutters"><div class="col-md-7 research-media dark"><video autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata"><source src="{{ '/assets/video/tmech_research_clip.mp4' | relative_url }}" type="video/mp4"></video></div><div class="col-md-5 d-flex align-items-center"><div class="card-body"><div class="research-text"><h5 class="card-title"><a href="{{ '/assets/pdf/Tanveer_TMECH2026_accepted_with_supplementary.pdf' | relative_url }}">Fluid–Structure Interaction-Based Design of Soft Actuators</a></h5><p class="card-text">Structural FEM neglects the hydrodynamic loads acting on soft fluidic actuators underwater and biases their predicted pressure–deformation response. <div class="card research-card mb-4"><div class="row no-gutters flex-md-row-reverse"><div class="col-md-7 research-media dark"><video autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata"><source src="{{ '/assets/video/rss_research_clip.mp4' | relative_url }}" type="video/mp4"></video></div><div class="col-md-5 d-flex align-items-center"><div class="card-body"><div class="research-text"><h5 class="card-title"><a href="https://anchored-rollouts.github.io/">Stable Long-Horizon Prediction for Continuum Robots</a></h5><p class="card-text">Multi-step prediction for continuum robots accumulates error under actuation distribution shift and destabilizes rollouts. We anchor predictions to a <b>learned equilibrium prior</b> through a contractive update and recover transient dynamics with a <b>feature-lifted residual model</b>. On 200-step rollouts of a tendon-driven 3D continuum arm, backbone and tip position RMSE decrease by <b>26%</b> and <b>27%</b>, respectively.</p></div></div></div></div></div>
<div class="card research-card mb-4"><div class="row no-gutters"><div class="col-md-7 research-media"><img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/electrosense_fig1.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Electrode layout and simulated electric field"></div><div class="col-md-5 d-flex align-items-center"><div class="card-body"><div class="research-text"><h5 class="card-title"><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=rdWW5xPrhN">Pre-Contact Electrosensing for Underwater Soft Grippers</a></h5><p class="card-text">Turbidity, glare, and occlusion degrade vision during underwater grasping, and soft grippers lack a cue prior to contact. We instrument an octopus-inspired soft gripper with <b>active electrosense</b>, measuring perturbations of an applied electric field through a discrete electrode layout. Tank experiments show <b>structured, object-dependent voltage changes</b> before contact.</p></div></div></div></div></div>
<div class="card research-card mb-4"><div class="card-body"><div class="research-text"><h5 class="card-title">Actuator-Effectiveness Estimation for Policy Recovery</h5><p class="card-text">Actuator loss of effectiveness breaks the action-to-motion map assumed by learned robot policies. We estimate a <b>continuous actuator-effectiveness factor</b> from observation–action history and condition a recovery module on it while the nominal policy remains frozen, reducing terminal error by up to <b>38.0%</b> in continuum robot reaching.</p></div></div></div>
<div class="card research-card mb-4"><div class="row no-gutters flex-md-row-reverse"><div class="col-md-7 research-media"><img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/lilab_swarm.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Swarm robotics at the Li Lab"></div><div class="col-md-5 d-flex align-items-center"><div class="card-body"><div class="research-text"><h5 class="card-title">World Models for Robot Swarms</h5><p class="card-text">We develop a <b>world model</b> for swarms of camera-equipped Raspberry Pi robots, implemented as a <b>spiking neural network</b> that runs onboard every robot and serves as the basis for decentralized control.</p></div></div></div></div></div>
<div class="card research-card mb-4"><div class="row no-gutters"><div class="col-md-7 research-media"><img src="{{ '/assets/img/research/lilab_fishvr.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Fish virtual reality setup at the Li Lab"></div><div class="col-md-5 d-flex align-items-center"><div class="card-body"><div class="research-text"><h5 class="card-title">System Identification of Fish Responses to a Virtual Conspecific</h5><p class="card-text">We identify <b>stimulus–response models</b> of zebrafish following a virtual fish under step heading changes, toward interpretable descriptions of the turning response and a controller that steers real fish in <b>closed loop</b>.</p></div></div></div></div></div>
