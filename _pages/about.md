I am currently a doctoral candidate under Prof. <a href='https://amr.pku.edu.cn/jzyg/szdw/X/0dba539cb6214815a8b90e659d9eabef.htm'>Guangming Xie</a> at the School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics, Peking University, and an incoming visiting research fellow at the Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior, hosted by Dr. <a href='https://www.ab.mpg.de/725930/dr-liang-li'>Liang Li</a>. I am also a <a href='https://www.uni-konstanz.de/zukunftskolleg/community/zukonnect-fellows/'>ZUKOnnect Fellow</a> at the Zukunftskolleg, affiliated with the Department of Biology/Centre for the Advanced Study of Collective Behaviour, University of Konstanz.

Previously, I was a lecturer at the <a href='https://www.au.edu.pk/pages/Faculties/IAA/Departments/MechanicalAerospace/FacultyDetail.aspx?fid=1545'>Institute of Avionics and Aeronautics (IAA)</a>, Air University, Islamabad. I obtained my master’s degree in mechanical engineering from the GIK Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology (GIKIEST), Swabi, in 2021. During my masters, I was affiliated with the Vibrational Dynamics and Controls (VDC) Lab, advised by Prof. <a href='https://cie.kfupm.edu.sa/people/faculty/dr-sarvat-m-ahmad/'>Sarvat M. Ahmad</a>. I received my bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from the University of Engineering & Technology (UET), Peshawar, in 2019, advised by Prof. <a href='https://www.kent.edu/trumbull/profile/mohammad-irfan-phd-cem'>Mohammad A. Irfan</a>. I have also worked at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan, and at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Intelligent Manufacturing and Robotics (IRC-IMR), King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, Saudi Arabia.

My research focuses on bio-inspired robotics, control, and robot learning, particularly for soft and continuum robots operating underwater. I am particularly interested in combining physics-based modeling, intelligent control, and multimodal sensing to improve the robustness, adaptability, and efficiency of robotic manipulation in challenging underwater environments.

Current and future directions of my work include:

1. Soft-fluidic actuator design through physics-based modeling, optimization, and data-driven methods
2. Modeling and control of highly nonlinear soft and continuum robotic systems for underwater manipulation
3. Robot learning and reinforcement learning for robust control, adaptation, and policy recovery
4. Bio-inspired sensing mechanisms, including electrosensing, for underwater perception and pre-contact interaction
5. Autonomous marine robotic systems with learning-enabled control and embodied intelligence

Please feel free to reach out, if you would like to talk about my work or discuss something of mutual interest.
