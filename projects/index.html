Fluid–Structure Interaction-Based Design of Soft Actuators

Structural FEM neglects the hydrodynamic loads acting on soft fluidic actuators underwater and biases their predicted pressure–deformation response. We embed two-way external fluid–structure interaction (FSI) simulation and non-parametric regression surrogates in the design loop. The FSI-optimized actuator achieves comparable bending at 30% lower pressure and a 46% larger bending angle than the FEM-optimized design.

Stable Long-Horizon Prediction for Continuum Robots

Multi-step prediction for continuum robots accumulates error under actuation distribution shift and destabilizes rollouts. We anchor predictions to a learned equilibrium prior through a contractive update and recover transient dynamics with a feature-lifted residual model. On 200-step rollouts of a tendon-driven 3D continuum arm, backbone and tip position RMSE decrease by 26% and 27%, respectively.

Pre-Contact Electrosensing for Underwater Soft Grippers

Turbidity, glare, and occlusion degrade vision during underwater grasping, and soft grippers lack a cue prior to contact. We instrument an octopus-inspired soft gripper with active electrosense, measuring perturbations of an applied electric field through a discrete electrode layout. Tank experiments show structured, object-dependent voltage changes before contact.

Actuator-Effectiveness Estimation for Policy Recovery

Actuator loss of effectiveness breaks the action-to-motion map assumed by learned robot policies. We estimate a continuous actuator-effectiveness factor from observation–action history and condition a recovery module on it while the nominal policy remains frozen, reducing terminal error by up to 38.0% in continuum robot reaching.

World Models for Robot Swarms

We develop a world model for swarms of camera-equipped Raspberry Pi robots, implemented as a spiking neural network that runs onboard every robot and serves as the basis for decentralized control.

System Identification of Fish Responses to a Virtual Conspecific

We identify stimulus–response models of zebrafish following a virtual fish under step heading changes, toward interpretable descriptions of the turning response and a controller that steers real fish in closed loop.

Collaborative Work

Bio-to-Robot Transfer of Fish Sensorimotor Dynamics

An interpretable ARX model maps bidirectionally between EMG and kinematics in freely swimming koi across laminar and vortex flows and transfers to a robotic fish.

EMG-Driven Telemetry for Freely Swimming Fish

A 16-channel telemetry unit records intramuscular EMG in freely swimming fish, from which body pose and flow conditions are decoded.

Proprioception from Energy-Harvesting Transducers

Piezoelectric transducers embedded in a passive soft hydrofoil for energy harvesting also provide proprioception, reconstructing the dynamic midline in flow with 0.68 mm RMSE using a bidirectional LSTM.
